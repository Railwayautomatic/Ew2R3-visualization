About the project

A visual route into a measurable relation

Ew2R3.org is the scientific and publication direction of the RWA® ecosystem. It develops open visualizations, calculations, papers, and testable research questions around E = ω²R³ as the worldview-correct form of Kepler’s third law.

a₁³/T₁² = a₂³/T₂²

For bodies orbiting the same central body, Kepler’s relation above is constant. With angular velocity ω = 2π/T, the same relation is written as E = ω²R³ = 4π²R³/T².

The site keeps three layers distinct: observed inputs, reproducible calculations, and proposed physical interpretation. Applied engineering directions are developed separately through WarpDrive.RWA.ua and through WarpDrive.RWA.bayern.

Current funding. As of 2026, the project is financed exclusively by the RWA® / RAILWAYAUTOMATIC® group of companies, which has supported this research direction since 2013. Voluntary donations are external support for the open visualization, translations, publications, and independent verification; they do not buy access or influence scientific conclusions.
